CFA Program · Level I

Full Mock Exam

A full-length practice exam in the format of the real one: 180 three-choice questions in two sessions of 135 minutes. Each attempt draws a fresh set from a pool of 2,130 questions, weighted by topic as in the exam.

How it worksSession 1 covers Ethics, Quantitative Methods, Economics, Financial Statement Analysis and Corporate Finance. Session 2 covers Equities, Fixed Income, Derivatives and Risk Management, Alternative Investments and Portfolio Construction. Topic weights are those in effect from February 2027. Answers and explanations appear when you finish a session, with a breakdown by topic. An unfinished session is kept on this device, so you can leave and come back; the clock keeps running.
