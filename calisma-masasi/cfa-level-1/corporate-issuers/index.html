CFA Program · Level I

Corporate Issuers

Seven readings, in curriculum order. Each opens with its key terms, explained in English and Turkish, followed by concise notes and three-choice practice questions. A 24-question timed mock exam is at the end.

Exam formatLevel I has 180 multiple-choice questions in two sessions of 135 minutes, about 90 seconds per question. Every question has three choices, A, B and C, and there is no penalty for a wrong answer. Corporate Issuers carries roughly 6–9% of the exam.
